# Generated image provenance

The individual art files documented here are concept artwork created with the built-in ImageGen tool. Public Google review photos and avatars remain attributed to their respective reviewers.

Project assets live in `apps/web/public/images/`. Transparent images were generated with alpha transparency; Sharp was used only for WebP/JPEG export, sizing and trimming transparent outer padding. Original PNGs remain under the local Codex generated-images directory.

The prompts preserve the original generation requests, including earlier proposed placements. The homepage now pairs its story introduction with the wok painting below.
