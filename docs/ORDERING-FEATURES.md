# Restaurant ordering feature checklist

This page tracks the supplied restaurant ordering brief against the Wok & Chop repository. The staff and guest order workflows use the local, unpaid preview. The restaurant API binds to loopback and rejects production mode. Restaurant launch requires a production service and acceptance testing.

| Area | Available in the local preview | Remaining work for launch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile storefront | Responsive home, menu, story and contact pages. Menu photos where available, instant search by name, heat and other known details, category filters and cart feedback. New pages and reloads open at the top. | Production hosting and real-device performance measurements. Add approved photos and verified ingredients, allergens and preparation estimates for the full menu. |
| Customization | Required choices and optional extras where configured, instant repricing, kitchen notes, line edits and opt-in saved dish choices on the current device. | Kitchen-approved ingredient removals, topping swaps and spice options for each dish. Allergy notes need the restaurant's handling procedures. |
| Guest checkout | Guests can review a server-priced pickup quote and submit a test order without an account. Earlier delivery receipts can repopulate the bag for pickup at current catalog prices. | Live direct pickup acceptance, guest-order claiming or receipt delivery, and configured Google, Apple and Facebook sign-in. |
| Payments | Food totals are calculated on the server. Direct pickup records an unpaid order for payment at the restaurant. Uber Eats handles payment for its own orders. | Approve direct pickup tax and in-store settlement rules. Provider-hosted payment for direct ordering needs a separate implementation and reconciliation. |
| Tracking | Private guest lookup codes, pickup progress, earlier delivery history, account receipts and staff status controls. Uber Eats provides tracking for orders placed there. | Customer notifications and verified pickup estimates. |
| Promotions and loyalty | Demo codes enforce minimums and discount caps. Staff reports show usage and food discounts for completed test deliveries. | Approved campaigns, start/end times, redemption limits, birthday and welcome offers, consent, personalization and loyalty tiers. |
| Menu and inventory | Manager-published PostgreSQL lunch catalog with descriptions, modifier selections and availability controls. Published prices apply to browsing and pickup quotes. Sold-out dishes are hidden from browsing. | Recipe and stock quantities, automatic deduction/restock rules, a timed category menu and holiday calendar. Native apps and delivery-platform menu sync need their own integrations. |
| Operator and kitchen | Manager sign-in protects the order desk, active and completed queues, kitchen display, pickup handoff, timestamps, customer order lookup and kitchen slips. Earlier deliveries retain driver slips. The printer setup page supports manual testing and dispatch through an installed Windows queue. | Production acceptance and auto-accept rules, order revisions after acceptance, kitchen priorities and qualification of the restaurant's actual printer. |
| Thermal print | Printer setup guide, virtual spool and supervised specimen test. | Connect the actual printer and verify readable paper output, failure recovery and automatic production routing on the selected model. See [the printer setup guide](../apps/print-bridge/README.md). |
| Reports | Manager reports for earlier deliveries show volumes, completion, top dishes, busiest Toronto hours, discounts and measured event intervals. | Direct pickup reporting, payment and cost data for financial/profit reports, and verified accuracy and satisfaction inputs. |
| Delivery logistics | Public delivery opens Uber Eats for ordering and dispatch. Earlier direct delivery tickets retain manual handoff. | Courier operations and service information are managed in Uber Eats. |
| Channels and locations | First-party pickup for the Kanata location and a link to Uber Eats for pickup or delivery. | Kiosk and table QR order modes, iOS and Android apps, and location-aware catalog and dispatch. |
| Security and privacy | PostgreSQL-backed accounts and order records, session-based roles, access-checked order lookups, and private tracking codes. The public privacy page explains the preview. | Production TLS, payment-provider qualification, privileged MFA, approved policies, account data export/deletion workflow and independent security and privacy review. |
| Support and guest feedback | Restaurant phone and email, Google review excerpts and links to the listing. | Automated post-delivery feedback, authorised review aggregation, chatbot and live agent handoff. |
| Notification preferences | Checkout supports optional pickup updates. Email offers have a separate guest signup, email confirmation and unsubscribe. Managers can preview offers and queue them for confirmed subscribers. | Configure Hostinger credentials and a live HTTPS website origin, then verify delivery, consent links and sender authentication. |
