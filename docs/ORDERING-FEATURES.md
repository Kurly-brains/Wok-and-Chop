# Restaurant ordering feature checklist

This page tracks the supplied restaurant ordering brief against the Wok & Chop repository. The staff and guest order workflows use the local, unpaid preview. The restaurant API binds to loopback and rejects production mode. Restaurant launch requires a production service and acceptance testing.

| Area | Available in the local preview | Remaining work for launch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile storefront | Responsive home, menu, story and contact pages. Menu photos where available, instant search by name, heat and other known details, category filters and cart feedback. New pages and reloads open at the top. | Production hosting and real-device performance measurements. Add approved photos and verified ingredients, allergens and preparation estimates for the full menu. |
| Customization | Required choices and optional extras where configured, instant repricing, kitchen notes, line edits and opt-in saved dish choices on the current device. | Kitchen-approved ingredient removals, topping swaps and spice options for each dish. Allergy notes need the restaurant's handling procedures. |
| Guest checkout | Guests can review a server-priced delivery quote and submit a test request without an account. Accounts can later view their own history and reorder eligible items using current catalog prices. | Live pickup and delivery acceptance, guest-order claiming or receipt delivery, and configured Google, Apple and Facebook sign-in. |
| Payments | Food totals are calculated on the server. The delivery workflow records unpaid test requests. | Configure and verify provider-hosted payments, wallet eligibility, PayPal and split-payment handling. Approve tax, refund and settlement rules, then test webhooks and reconciliation. |
| Tracking | Private guest tracking code, delivery progress and history, account receipts and staff status controls. | Transactional push, text or email delivery, a courier location source, production ETA calculation and map tracking. |
| Promotions and loyalty | Demo codes enforce minimums and discount caps. Staff reports show usage and food discounts for completed test deliveries. | Approved campaigns, start/end times, redemption limits, birthday and welcome offers, consent, personalization and loyalty tiers. |
| Menu and inventory | Manager-published PostgreSQL lunch catalog with descriptions, modifier selections and availability controls. Published prices apply to browsing and delivery quotes. Sold-out dishes are hidden from browsing. | Recipe and stock quantities, automatic deduction/restock rules, a timed category menu and holiday calendar. Native apps and delivery-platform menu sync need their own integrations. |
| Operator and kitchen | Manager delivery desk, active and completed queues, kitchen display, timestamps, customer order lookup and printable operational slips. A training board includes synthetic orders. | Production acceptance and auto-accept rules, order revisions after acceptance, kitchen priorities and durable routing to production print jobs. |
| Thermal print | Printer setup guide, virtual spool and supervised specimen test. | Connect the actual printer and verify readable paper output, failure recovery and automatic production routing on the selected model. See [the printer setup guide](../apps/print-bridge/README.md). |
| Reports | Manager reports show volumes, completion, top dishes, busiest Toronto hours, discounts and measured event intervals. | Payment and cost data for financial/profit reports, plus verified accuracy and satisfaction inputs. |
| Delivery logistics | Configurable Ontario postal-prefix validation and manual handoff from preparation to delivery. | Approved zones, per-zone fees, minimums and estimates, pickup fallback, driver assignments, GPS, geofencing, live routing, batching and driver performance. |
| Channels and locations | First-party website for the Kanata location. | Kiosk and table QR order modes, iOS and Android apps, location-aware catalog/dispatch, and approved Uber Eats and DoorDash connections. These need dedicated operational rules and provider access. |
| Security and privacy | PostgreSQL-backed accounts and order records, session-based roles, access-checked order lookups, and private tracking codes. The public privacy page explains the preview. | Production TLS, payment-provider qualification, privileged MFA, approved policies, account data export/deletion workflow and independent security and privacy review. |
| Support and guest feedback | Restaurant phone and email, Google review excerpts and links to the listing. | Automated post-delivery feedback, authorised review aggregation, chatbot and live agent handoff. |
| Notification preferences | Customers can return to their order tracker. | Order update opt-ins, separate marketing consent, channel preferences and access to a production send provider. |
